## 1. Project Overview

[Redacted] City, one of India's 100 Smart Cities, is seeking a technology partner for Phase 2 of its digital infrastructure initiative. Phase 1 (completed in 2024) established the foundational digital backbone.

### Phase 2 Objectives:
- Integrate 40+ government departments into a unified digital service delivery platform
- Implement AI-driven urban management systems
- Deploy IoT sensors across 200+ city locations for real-time monitoring
- Create a citizen engagement platform with 10+ services
- Enable data-driven decision making for municipal authorities

### Section B: Solution Requirements

**S-001: Integrated Command & Control Center (ICCC)**
- Real-time monitoring of city infrastructure (traffic, water, electricity, waste)
- AI-based predictive alerts for congestion and maintenance
- Disaster management module with emergency response integration

**S-002: Citizen Service Platform**
- Mobile-first application for 200+ citizen services
- Integrated payment gateway (UPI, credit/debit cards)
- Grievance redressal tracking system
- Multi-lingual support (English, Hindi, regional language)
- Aadhaar-based authentication

**S-003: Smart Traffic Management**
- AI-powered traffic flow optimization
- Integration with 500+ traffic signals
- Smart parking with real-time availability
- e-Challan integration with transport department

**S-004: Smart Environment Monitoring**
- IoT sensors for air quality (AQI) across 25 locations
- Water quality monitoring for 10 water bodies
- Noise pollution monitoring in commercial zones
- Weather prediction integration (IMD APIs)

**S-005: Smart Waste Management**
- GPS tracking for 500+ garbage collection vehicles
- Route optimization using AI
- IoT-enabled smart bins with fill-level sensors
- Citizen reporting for waste collection issues

**S-006: Cybersecurity Operations Center (C-SOC)**
- 24x7 monitoring of all city systems
- Threat intelligence integration
- Compliance reporting (DPDP Act, IT Act)
- Incident response and breach notification

## 5. GAPS (Requirements We Cannot Satisfy)

**GAP-001**: IoT sensor network with 200+ locations - Our IoT practice is still developing; current experience is limited to 5-10 sensors for proof-of-concept. We lack the field engineering capability for city-wide IoT deployment.

**GAP-002**: Smart waste management with route optimization - This specific requirement requires domain expertise in municipal waste management that we do not have. Our expertise is in enterprise IT and digital transformation, not physical operations.

**GAP-003**: e-Challan integration - We have no prior experience with government traffic enforcement systems.
