## 1. Client Brief

CareWell Health Systems is a leading healthcare provider in India with 35 hospitals, 200+ clinics, and 15,000+ beds across 50 cities. We serve 2.5 million patients annually and are in the process of becoming India's largest integrated healthcare network.

### Business Context

The healthcare industry is undergoing rapid digital transformation, driven by:
- Post-COVID acceleration of telemedicine
- Government push for digital health records (ABDM/ABHA)
- Increasing patient expectations for consumer-grade digital experiences
- Need for operational efficiency with rising costs

### Strategic Imperatives

CareWell's board has approved a ₹500 Crore digital transformation initiative over 3 years. Key strategic goals:
1. **Patient First**: Create a unified digital patient experience across all touchpoints
2. **Operational Excellence**: Reduce administrative costs by 30%
3. **Clinical Outcomes**: Leverage data for improved treatment outcomes
4. **Regulatory Compliance**: Achieve 100% compliance with HIPAA and DPDP Act

## 2. Key Requirements

### 2.1 Deliverables Required

**D-001: Digital Patient Onboarding Platform**
We need an integrated solution that eliminates paper-based registration. The solution should:
- Enable patients to pre-register via mobile/web before visiting
- Integrate with ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) for unified health records
- Support OPD/IPD admission workflows with minimal manual intervention
- Provide self-service kiosks at hospitals for walk-in patients

**D-002: Unified Electronic Health Records (EHR)**
Our current EHR systems are fragmented across 3 different platforms. We need:
- A single, unified patient record accessible across all CareWell facilities
- Integration with diagnostic labs, pharmacies, and imaging centers
- AI-powered clinical decision support for doctors
- Patient-controlled data sharing with family members and external providers

**D-003: Telemedicine & Remote Monitoring Platform**
- Virtual consultation capabilities with video, audio, and chat
- Remote patient monitoring (RPM) for chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension)
- Integration with wearable devices (smartwatches, glucose monitors, BP cuffs)
- AI-based triage for emergency cases

**D-004: Predictive Analytics & Operational Intelligence**
- Bed availability forecasting
- Patient no-show prediction
- Resource optimization (staff, equipment, OR scheduling)
- Population health analytics for preventive care

**D-005: Cybersecurity & Compliance Framework**
- HIPAA-compliant data handling
- SOC2 Type II certification
- End-to-end encryption for all patient data
- Zero-trust architecture implementation
- Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
