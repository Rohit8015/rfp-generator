# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL - RFP-A-2026-001
## Digital Transformation Partner for NBFC

**Issued By:** Akshaya Finance Limited (AFL)
**Issue Date:** March 15, 2026
**Submission Deadline:** April 30, 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Akshaya Finance Limited (AFL) is a 25-year-old NBFC with a balance sheet of ₹42,000 Crores, serving 8.5 million customers across 200+ cities in India. We are seeking a digital transformation partner to modernize our customer acquisition and servicing platforms.

**Key Objectives:**
- 40% reduction in customer onboarding time
- 50% increase in digital sales conversion
- 95% automation of manual touchpoints
- Implementation of AI-driven credit scoring

---

## 2. Scope of Work

### 2.1 Mandatory Requirements (SHALL/REQUIRED)

**R-001**: The vendor SHALL implement a unified customer onboarding platform supporting KYC, Aadhaar eSign, and PAN verification.

**R-002**: The vendor SHALL provide a mobile-first loan origination system (LOS) with offline capability.

**R-003**: The vendor SHALL integrate with existing CBS (Core Banking System) and data warehouse.

**R-004**: The vendor SHALL implement real-time fraud detection using AI/ML models.

**R-005**: The vendor SHALL ensure 99.95% system availability for customer-facing applications.

**R-006**: The vendor SHALL comply with RBI guidelines on digital lending (2022) and Data Protection Bill.

**R-007**: The vendor SHALL provide a comprehensive data migration strategy from legacy systems.

**R-008**: The vendor SHALL conduct security audits and penetration testing quarterly.

**R-009**: The vendor SHALL maintain a dedicated 24x7 support team for critical issues.

**R-010**: The vendor SHALL provide source code escrow for all custom developments.

**R-011**: The vendor SHALL implement role-based access control (RBAC) with audit trails.

**R-012**: The vendor SHALL deliver comprehensive documentation (Technical, User, Admin).

### 2.2 Weighted Requirements (Should/Scored - 100 points total)

| Requirement | Weight | Description |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| R-013 | 20 | **MOST IMPORTANT**: Should provide AI-based predictive lead scoring |
| R-014 | 15 | Should offer pre-built integrations with fintech ecosystems (UPI, BBPS) |
| R-015 | 15 | Should include a customer analytics dashboard with 50+ KPIs |
| R-016 | 12 | Should provide automated regulatory reporting (RBI returns) |
| R-017 | 10 | Should offer chatbot/voicebot integration for customer service |
| R-018 | 8 | Should include social media listening and sentiment analysis |
| R-019 | 8 | Should support multi-lingual interface (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada) |
| R-020 | 6 | Should provide mobile app with biometric authentication |
| R-021 | 6 | Should offer document management with AI-based classification |

### 2.3 Nice-to-Have Requirements (May/Preferred)

**R-022**: May include blockchain-based document verification

**R-023**: May provide gamification features for customer engagement

**R-024**: May offer integration with CIBIL/Experian APIs

**R-025**: May include AR/VR for product demonstrations

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Evaluation Method |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Technical Solution | 35% | Workshop presentation |
| Implementation Approach | 20% | Project plan review |
| Team Expertise | 15% | CVs and interviews |

**Scoring Matrix**:
- Mandatory Requirements: Pass/Fail (all MUST pass)
- Weighted Requirements: 1-10 scoring per requirement, multiplied by weight
- Nice-to-Have: Bonus points (max 10 extra)
