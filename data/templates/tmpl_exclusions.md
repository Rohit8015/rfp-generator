# Exclusions

## Scope Exclusions

The following items are explicitly excluded from the scope of this proposal unless separately specified and priced:

### 1. Hardware & Infrastructure

- Provisioning, procurement, or installation of any hardware (servers, networking equipment, storage, etc.)
- Datacenter setup or management (whether on-premises or co-location)
- Client-side infrastructure (desktops, laptops, mobile devices, etc.)
- Cabling, racks, power, cooling, or any datacenter physical infrastructure
- Equipment warranty or maintenance (covered by hardware vendors)
- Third-party cloud infrastructure costs (subscriptions to AWS/Azure/GCP are client responsibility)

### 2. Software Licenses

- Licensing costs for Commercial-Off-The-Shelf (COTS) software
- Licensing costs for third-party tools, frameworks, and libraries (unless open source)
- Licensing costs for development tools (IDEs, testing tools, etc.)
- Microsoft, Oracle, SAP, or any other enterprise software licensing
- Operating system licenses (Windows, Linux, etc.)
- Database licenses (unless cloud-provider managed services)

### 3. Third-Party Services

- Cost of third-party services, APIs, or data feeds
- Integration with third-party systems beyond those explicitly identified
- Customization of third-party applications (unless separately priced)
- Maintenance or support of third-party applications
- Service level agreements with third-party providers

### 4. Training

- Training beyond the number of sessions and participants specified in the SOW
- Training materials beyond those specified
- Train-the-trainer programs (unless separately requested)
- Refresher training after project closure
- Training infrastructure (labs, machines, software licenses for training)
- Certification exams and fees

### 5. Support & Maintenance

- Application maintenance or support beyond the defined project support period
- Post-go-live enhancements (unless covered by AMC)
- Bug fixes after the warranty period
- Operational support after stabilization
- Database administration and maintenance
- Infrastructure monitoring and management (unless covered by managed services)
- Performance optimization after go-live (unless covered by AMC)
- Security patching after the project duration

### 6. Data Migration

- Data migration beyond systems and data specified in the scope
- Data cleansing beyond defined scope
- Data archival or decommissioning (unless separately specified)
- Third-party data migration tools (unless open source)
- Data recovery or backup beyond what is specified
- Data validation beyond defined scope
- Historical data migration beyond defined period

### 7. Change Management

- Change management beyond defined scope (e.g., departmental changes outside project scope)
- User adoption after the project closure
- Organization restructuring or redesign
- Policy or process changes beyond project scope
- End-user communication beyond defined plan
- Employee retention or HR-related changes

### 8. Operations

- Day-to-day operations after project completion
- BPO or KPO services
- Facilities management
- Customer support (unrelated to the platform)
- Regulatory filing and compliance monitoring (unless specified)
- Physical operations (warehouse, inventory, logistics)

## Commercial Exclusions

1. **Travel & Accommodation**: Expenses for travel to client sites are excluded unless specified.

2. **Visa & Work Permits**: Obtaining visas or work permits for non-Indian citizens is excluded.

3. **Insurance**: Client-specific insurance coverage is excluded (client to maintain own coverage).

4. **Legal Fees**: Legal advisory or legal representation fees are excluded.

5. **Third-Party Audits**: Any third-party audit costs (security, compliance) are excluded.

6. **Currency Fluctuation**: Impact of currency fluctuation beyond fixed exchange rate is excluded.

7. **Force Majeure**: Costs arising from events beyond our control (natural disasters, pandemics, etc.)

8. **Penalties**: Any client-imposed penalties beyond the terms of the contract (specific penalties will be mutually agreed).

## Technical Exclusions

1. **Customization**: Any customization beyond what is clearly defined in the scope.

2. **Decommissioning**: Decommissioning of legacy systems (unless separately specified).

3. **System Administration**: General system administration beyond project needs.

4. **Database Design**: Database design beyond what is required for the project scope.

5. **Network Design**: Network design beyond what is required for the solution.

6. **Security Monitoring**: Ongoing security monitoring (beyond the project duration).

7. **Disaster Recovery**: DR setup (unless separately specified and priced).

8. **Load Testing**: Load testing at scale (beyond what is defined in the test plan).

## Client Responsibility Exclusions

1. **Decision-Making**: Client's internal decision-making processes and delays are excluded.

2. **Policy Changes**: Changes in client policies during the project are excluded.

3. **Staff Changes**: Client staff changes and transitions are excluded.

4. **Internal Politics**: Client internal politics or organizational changes are excluded.

5. **Regulatory Environment**: Changes in regulations or regulatory environment are excluded.

## Risk Exclusions

1. **External Events**: Disruptions from events beyond our control (pandemics, natural disasters, geopolitical events).

2. **Force Majeure**: As defined in the standard contract.

3. **Third-Party Failures**: Failures of third-party systems or providers.

4. **Client Delays**: Delays caused by client actions or inactions.

5. **Scope Creep**: Any work outside approved scope without formal change order.

## Termination Exclusions

1. **Early Termination Fees**: Client may terminate with 30-day notice (or as per contract).

2. **Transition Costs**: Client is responsible for costs of transitioning to another provider.

3. **Archiving Costs**: Client is responsible for archiving or backup of project artifacts.

4. **Data Portability**: Data exported in standard formats (custom formats may incur charges).

## Intellectual Property Exclusions

1. **Pre-existing IP**: Client pre-existing intellectual property remains with the client.

2. **Third-Party IP**: Third-party IP used in the solution remains with the respective owners.

3. **Open Source**: Open-source components are subject to their respective licenses.

4. **Generic Components**: Reusable components developed by us (outside this project) remain our IP.

## Liability Exclusions

1. **Consequential Damages**: We are not liable for consequential or indirect damages.

2. **Lost Profits**: We are not liable for lost profits or revenue.

3. **Third-Party Claims**: We are not liable for claims from third parties.

4. **Client Decisions**: We are not liable for decisions made by client based on our recommendations.

5. **Unforeseen Circumstances**: We are not liable for unforeseen circumstances beyond our control.

**Note:** Any exclusions listed here can be included at additional cost through a formal change order or SOW amendment.
