# Assumptions

## Commercial Assumptions

1. **Pricing Validity**: The pricing in this proposal is valid for 90 days from the date of submission.

2. **Taxes**: GST at 18% and any other applicable taxes will be charged extra as per government regulations.

3. **Currency**: All pricing is in Indian Rupees (INR). For international payments, exchange rate of ₹83 = $1 will be used.

4. **Inflation**: For contracts exceeding 12 months, pricing adjustments of up to 5% per annum may apply.

5. **Travel Expenses**: Client will reimburse travel, accommodation, and per diem expenses at actuals, where applicable.

6. **Billing**: Invoices will be raised monthly (for T&M) or on milestone achievement (for fixed price) with 30-day payment terms.

7. **Resource Billing**: All resources will be billed based on actual hours worked, tracked through our timesheet system.

8. **Holidays**: Indian public holidays and client holidays will be non-billable days.

## Technical Assumptions

1. **Infrastructure**: Client will provide all necessary infrastructure (hardware, network, cloud resources) required for the solution.

2. **Access**: Client will provide timely access to all stakeholders, systems, data, and documentation required for the project.

3. **Integration**: Client's existing systems and APIs will be available as per the agreed schedule and will function as documented.

4. **Data Quality**: Client data provided for migration, testing, or analysis will be complete, accurate, and fit for purpose.

5. **Security**: Client will maintain and enforce security policies that are compatible with our security framework.

6. **Documentation**: Client will provide all necessary documentation for existing systems, processes, and data models.

7. **Third-Party Dependencies**: All third-party tools, libraries, and services will be licensed appropriately by the client.

8. **Testing Data**: Client will provide representative test data that is anonymized/scrubbed for sensitive information.

## Project Assumptions

1. **Scope**: Work is limited to the scope clearly defined in this SOW and associated documents.

2. **Timeline**: Project timeline is based on client's ability to provide inputs, decisions, and approvals on time.

3. **Decision-Making**: Client decisions and approvals will be provided within 5 business days of request.

4. **Team Stability**: Key project resources will remain assigned for the duration of the project.

5. **Governance**: A joint steering committee will guide the program and resolve issues.

6. **Change Management**: Any changes to scope, timeline, or budget will be managed through formal change control.

7. **Communication**: Project status and critical issues will be communicated through agreed governance channels.

8. **SLA**: Service levels and penalties will be as defined in the Service Level Agreement (attached separately).

## Regulatory & Legal Assumptions

1. **Compliance**: Client is compliant with all applicable regulations (RBI, DPDP, HIPAA, IT Act, etc.).

2. **Licenses**: Client holds all necessary licenses, permits, and approvals required for their business operations.

3. **Data Privacy**: Client has obtained all necessary consents for personal data processing.

4. **Legal Review**: Client legal team has reviewed and agrees to all terms in the Master Services Agreement.

5. **Export Control**: No export-controlled data, technology, or software will be involved in the project.

6. **Intellectual Property**: All existing client IP remains the property of the client. All new IP will be owned as per the IP clause.

7. **Confidentiality**: Both parties will sign and adhere to the Non-Disclosure Agreement.

8. **Insurance**: Client maintains appropriate business and professional liability insurance.

## Risk Assumptions

1. **External Factors**: Project timeline is contingent on no significant external disruptions (pandemics, natural disasters, political instability).

2. **Regulatory Changes**: Any substantive changes in regulatory requirements will be handled through scope change.

3. **Client Decisions**: Client decision-making will not cause unreasonable delays.

4. **Third-Party Performance**: Any delays from third-party dependencies are beyond our control.

5. **Scope Creep**: Unapproved scope changes will be handled through formal change control.

6. **Resourcing**: We will maintain at least 15% bench strength to address any unplanned attrition.

7. **Go-Live**: Client will ensure readiness for go-live including training, data readiness, and user adoption.

8. **Post-Go-Live**: Client will provide necessary support during hyper-care phase.

## Client Assumptions

Based on client's requirements and provided documentation, we assume:

1. The client's current-state documentation is accurate and complete.
2. The client's business processes are as described in the current-state assessment.
3. The client's regulatory compliance status is as declared.
4. The client has the necessary IT and business resources available for the project.
5. The client's willingness for change and adoption is as demonstrated in the workshop.

## Mutual Assumptions

1. Both parties will work in good faith to achieve project objectives.
2. Both parties will communicate openly and transparently.
3. Both parties will escalate issues promptly through agreed governance channels.
4. Both parties will adhere to the agreed project governance.
5. Both parties will maintain confidentiality of all shared information.
6. Both parties will comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
7. Both parties will respect intellectual property rights.
8. Both parties will treat each other's employees with respect and professionalism.

---
**Note:** Any changes to these assumptions require formal amendment and mutual agreement.
