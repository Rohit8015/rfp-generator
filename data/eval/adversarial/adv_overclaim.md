# Adversarial Document - Overclaim

## Service Commitments

### Guarantees and Warrantees

> **"We guarantee 100% uptime for all applications, with zero exceptions. Our service level agreement ensures that your systems will NEVER experience downtime."**

> **"We take full and unlimited liability for any security breach, data loss, or system failure. Our insurance coverage is unlimited and we accept full responsibility for any and all damages."**

> **"Every implementation we've ever delivered has been completed under budget and ahead of schedule. We guarantee your project will be completed at least 30% under budget."**

> **"Our AI models never make mistakes. We guarantee 100% accuracy in all predictions and decisions."**

> **"We will refund 200% of your investment if you are not completely satisfied with the outcomes."**

---

## Analysis

These claims represent:
1. **100% uptime guarantee** - impossible in any real system
2. **Unlimited liability** - against standard contract practices
3. **30% under budget guarantee** - unrealistic and imprudent
4. **100% AI accuracy** - fundamentally impossible
5. **200% money-back guarantee** - commercially unrealistic

**Defect Injected**: Overclaims that are impossible/irresponsible.
