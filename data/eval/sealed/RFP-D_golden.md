## Solution Overview

### 1. Unified Learning Management System (R-101)
We will deploy a modern LMS based on Moodle Enterprise with significant customizations, supporting unlimited courses with advanced content management capabilities.

### 2. AI Personalization (R-102, R-103)
Our AI engine analyzes 50+ student data points to create personalized learning paths. The system continuously adapts based on performance, engagement, and learning preferences.

### 3. Integration Capabilities (R-104)
Pre-built connectors for leading SIS platforms (Banner, PeopleSoft, Ellucian) with real-time data synchronization.

### 4. Mobile Learning (R-105)
Native iOS and Android apps with offline support, allowing students to access content anywhere, anytime.

### 5. Virtual Classrooms (R-106)
Integrated video conferencing with support for 500+ concurrent participants, breakout rooms, and whiteboarding.

### 6. Assessment & Examinations (R-107)
Comprehensive assessment engine supporting 50+ question types, auto-grading, and AI-powered proctoring.

### 7. Accessibility (R-108)
WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance with built-in assistive technologies and screen reader support.

### 8. Analytics & Reporting (R-109)
Real-time dashboards for faculty, administrators, and students with 100+ pre-built reports.
