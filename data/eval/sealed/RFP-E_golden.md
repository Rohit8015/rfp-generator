## Solution Overview

### 1. Customer 360 View (R-201)
We will implement a master data management system creating a single source of truth for customer data across all banking systems.

### 2. AI Personalization (R-202, R-203)
Our AI engine analyzes 80+ customer attributes to deliver real-time personalized offers, content, and next-best-actions across channels.

### 3. Integration Capabilities (R-204)
Pre-built connectors for core banking systems (TCS BaNCS, Finacle), transactional systems, and third-party data sources.

### 4. Real-Time Engagement (R-205)
Event-driven architecture enabling real-time customer engagement triggered by 100+ events (transactions, behaviors, lifecycle milestones).

### 5. Customer Analytics (R-206, R-207)
Advanced analytics platform with 200+ KPIs, predictive models, and self-service dashboards for business users.

### 6. Campaign Management (R-208)
Omnichannel campaign management with AI-powered audience selection, channel optimization, and real-time performance tracking.

### 7. Data Privacy (R-209)
Built-in DPDP Act compliance with granular consent management, data anonymization, and audit trails.

### 8. Segmentation & Targeting (R-210)
AI-powered customer segmentation with 50+ segment types and dynamic segment updates based on real-time behavior.
