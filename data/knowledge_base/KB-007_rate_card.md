## Engagement Models & Pricing

### Model 1: Fixed Price (Project-Based)
- **Best for**: Well-defined projects with clear scope
- **Pricing**: Fixed cost based on effort estimation
- **Payment**: Milestone-based (30% advance, 70% based on milestones)
- **Change Management**: Formal change order process
- **Typical Range**: ₹50 Lakhs - ₹50 Crores

### Model 2: Time & Material (T&M)
- **Best for**: Evolving requirements, Agile development
- **Pricing**: Hourly rates as per role blend
- **Payment**: Monthly billing based on actual hours
- **Adjustments**: Quarterly rate adjustments based on market conditions
- **Typical Range**: ₹15 Lakhs - ₹15 Crores per month

### Model 3: Outcome-Based
- **Best for**: Strategic digital transformation partnerships
- **Pricing**: Base fee + success fee linked to KPIs
- **KPIs**: Revenue growth, cost reduction, customer satisfaction, etc.
- **Payment**: Monthly fixed fee + quarterly performance bonus
- **Typical Range**: ₹2 Crores - ₹20 Crores annually

### Model 4: Managed Services
- **Best for**: Application support, IT operations
- **Pricing**: Fixed monthly fee per resource
- **SLA**: Defined SLAs with penalties
- **Payment**: Monthly subscription
- **Typical Range**: ₹10 Lakhs - ₹5 Crores per month

## Special Pricing

### Volume Discounts
- 5% discount for annual contracts
- 10% discount for multi-year contracts (3+ years)
- Negotiable for large-scale programs (>₹50 Crores)

### Preferred Partnership Discounts
- 5% additional discount for preferred partner status
- Access to innovation labs and POCs at no cost
- Dedicated account management
- Quarterly innovation workshops

### Government Projects
- Minimum 5-10% cost advantage
- As per GeM (Government e-Marketplace) rates
- GST applicable as per government norms

## Billing & Payment Terms

### Standard Terms
- **Invoice**: Monthly (for T&M) or Milestone-based (for Fixed Price)
- **Payment Period**: 30 days from invoice date
- **Late Payment**: 1.5% interest per month
- **Retention**: 10% held for final acceptance (released after project closure)

### Tax Treatment
- **GST**: 18% applicable on all services
- **TDS**: As per applicable IT Act rates
- **International Payments**: Subject to FEMA regulations

## Contingency Policy

### Budget Contingency
- **Standard**: 10% of project budget for unplanned scope
- **Approval**: Managed through change control
- **Escalation**: Exceeding 15% requires steering committee approval

### Timeline Contingency
- **Standard**: 15% buffer in project schedule
- **Usage**: Requires PMO approval
- **Tracking**: Monitored in weekly reviews
