# C4 Consulting - Company Profile

## Who We Are

C4 Consulting is a leading Indian IT consulting and digital transformation firm headquartered in Mumbai, with 25+ branch offices across India and 15,000+ employees. Established in 2005, we've grown to become a trusted partner for enterprises, government agencies, and non-profits undergoing digital transformation.

**Key Facts:**
- Founded: 2005
- Headquarters: Mumbai, India
- Offices: 25+ locations across India
- Employees: 15,000+
- Revenue (FY25): ₹3,200 Crores
- Clients: 500+ active, including 150+ Fortune India 500 companies
- Certifications: ISO 27001, SOC2 Type II, CMMI Level 5
