# Resourcing & Staffing Model

## Overview

Our resourcing model ensures the right talent is available at the right time and cost, balancing offshore/onsite capabilities with specialized skills.

## Resource Pyramid

### Tier 1: Global Delivery Centers (70% of resources)
**Location**: Pune, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai
**Role Mix**: 
- 50% Junior Developers (0-3 years)
- 30% Mid Developers (3-8 years) 
- 15% Senior Developers (8-12 years)
- 5% Principal Architects (12+ years)

**Cost Advantage**: 40-50% lower than onsite

### Tier 2: Client Onsite (20% of resources)
**Location**: Client offices across India
**Role Mix**:
- 20% Project Managers
- 30% Business Analysts
- 30% Solution Architects
- 20% Change Management/Training

**Benefits**: Stakeholder alignment, rapid decision-making

### Tier 3: Nearshore (10% of resources)
**Location**: Mumbai, Delhi, other metro cities
**Role Mix**:
- Subject Matter Experts
- Senior Leadership
- Specialized skills (SAP, AI/ML, Cybersecurity)

## Staffing Process

### Resource Request Process
1. **Resource Request Form**: Submitted to RM (Resource Management)
2. **Approval**: PMO and Finance approval
3. **Sourcing**: Internal bench vs. external hiring
4. **Vetting**: Technical assessment and client interview
5. **Onboarding**: Compliance, security, project induction

### Resource Sourcing Channels
- **Internal Bench**: 15% of resources
- **Trained Freshers**: 20% from our training program
- **Experienced Hiring**: 40% from open market
- **Partners/Subcontractors**: 15% for specialized skills
- **Campus Hiring**: 10% from top engineering colleges

## Subcontracting Policy

### When We Use Subcontractors
- Niche skills not available internally
- Peak demand beyond internal capacity
- Specialized domain expertise (e.g., medical devices)
- Geographic coverage limitations

### Governance
- **Due Diligence**: Security, compliance, financial stability
- **Contract**: Standard DTA (Delivery Team Agreement)
- **Oversight**: Managed by our PMO
- **Quality**: Same standards as internal resources
- **Billing**: Markup capped at 15%

## Attrition Management

### Retention Programs
- **Competitive Compensation**: Annual market reviews
- **Growth Path**: Clear career progression
- **Learning**: 5% time for learning and innovation
- **Recognition**: Awards and incentives
- **Work-Life**: Flexible working, remote options

### Mitigation Strategies
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Documented processes
- **Succession Planning**: Identified backups
- **Multiple Points**: No single point of failure
- **Bench Strength**: 15% bench maintained
- **Rapid Backfill**: 30-day replacement guarantee
