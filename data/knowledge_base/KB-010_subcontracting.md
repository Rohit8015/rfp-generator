# Subcontracting and Third-Party Engagement Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs the engagement of subcontractors and third parties by C4 Consulting to ensure consistent quality, compliance, and client satisfaction.

## When Subcontracting is Permitted

### Acceptable Use Cases
1. **Specialized Skills**: Niche technologies not available in-house (e.g., legacy COBOL, specialized medical devices)
2. **Capacity Management**: Temporary peak demand beyond internal capacity
3. **Geographic Coverage**: Client locations where we don't have an office
4. **Cost Optimization**: Lower-cost locations (tier 2/3 cities)
5. **IP Independence**: Components not core to our IP

### Unacceptable Use Cases
1. **Core Competency**: Our primary capabilities (Digital Transformation, Cloud, AI/ML)
2. **Strategic Clients**: Without prior client approval
3. **Security-Sensitive**: Client data processing without client consent
4. **Loss-Leader**: Subcontracting at a loss to win deals

## Subcontractor Selection Criteria

### Mandatory Requirements
- **Legal Entity**: Registered company with PAN, GST
- **Financial Stability**: Audited financial statements
- **Security Compliance**: ISO 27001 or equivalent
- **Track Record**: 3+ years in similar work
- **Insurance**: Professional indemnity insurance

### Additional Evaluation
- **Technical Capability**: Skills assessment and certifications
- **Resource Pool**: Number and quality of resources
- **Past Performance**: Reference checks
- **Culture Alignment**: Work ethics and values
- **Pricing**: Competitive with our cost structure

## Subcontractor Governance

### Engagement Framework

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Contract Type** | Standard Subcontractor Agreement (SSA) |
| **Duration** | Project-based (6-24 months typical) |
| **Payment Terms** | 30 days from invoice, linked to deliverables |
| **Non-Solicitation** | 24-month restriction on hiring our employees |
| **IP Ownership** | All IP vests with C4 Consulting |
| **Confidentiality** | Standard NDA with penalties |

### Governance Structure
- **Account Manager**: Primary point of contact
- **PMO Oversight**: Weekly status reviews
- **Quality Audits**: Monthly, random audits
- **Performance Reviews**: Quarterly formal review
- **Escalation Matrix**: Defined for issues

### Flow-Down Obligations
Subcontractors must flow-down:
1. Security and compliance obligations
2. Confidentiality and data protection
3. Quality standards and SLAs
4. Reporting and governance requirements
5. Termination and transition provisions

## Subcontractor Management Process

### 1. Onboarding (2-4 weeks)
- Due diligence and background check
- Contract signing and legal review
- Security compliance audit
- System access provisioning
- Induction and training

### 2. Active Management (Throughout)
- Weekly status reports
- Monthly quality reviews
- Quarterly performance evaluation
- Compliance monitoring
- Risk assessment

### 3. Offboarding (2-4 weeks)
- Knowledge transfer plan
- System access revocation
- Documented handover
- Final quality audit
- Lessons learned

## Performance Metrics

### Service Level Agreements
- **Quality**: Defect rate <3%, 100% compliance with standards
- **Timeliness**: >95% on-time delivery
- **Responsiveness**: Issues resolved within SLA
- **Satisfaction**: >85% client satisfaction

### Penalties
- **Non-Performance**: Payment reduction based on severity
- **Security Breach**: Liquidated damages up to contract value
- **Client Dissatisfaction**: Termination with 30-day notice
- **Quality Issues**: Remediation at subcontractor's cost

## Risk Management

### Key Risks
1. **Quality Risk**: Inconsistent standards
   - **Mitigation**: Regular audits, quality gates
2. **Security Risk**: Data breach
   - **Mitigation**: Contracts, certifications, monitoring
3. **Resource Risk**: Attrition at subcontractor
   - **Mitigation**: Replacement guarantee (30 days)
4. **Transition Risk**: Subcontractor failure
   - **Mitigation**: Documented handover, exit plan

### Escalation Process
| Issue | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| Minor Quality | Project Manager | Program Manager | - |
| Major Quality | Program Manager | Delivery Head | Client |
| Security Concern | CISO | CEO | Board |
| Contract Breach | Legal Team | CFO | CEO |

## Client Transparency

### Client Consent Required For
- Subcontracting on strategic engagements
- Access to client data
- Client site presence
- Any subcontractor change

### Client Communication
- Quarterly reporting on subcontractor performance
- Immediate notification of subcontractor issues
- Access to subcontractor audits upon request
- No client is engaged without visibility into subcontracting arrangement

## Compliance & Reporting

### Regulatory Compliance
- DPDP Act: Data processing obligations
- RBI Guidelines: For financial services
- HIPAA: For healthcare (with BAAs)
- IT Act: Cybersecurity

### Reports
- **Monthly**: Performance dashboards
- **Quarterly**: Quality and compliance review
- **Annually**: Renewal evaluation
